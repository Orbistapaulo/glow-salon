Booking policy

Simple rules that keep everyone's time fair

These help us stay on schedule, so no one waits longer than they should.

Arriving

Please arrive 10 minutes before your appointment. It gives us time to check you in and talk through what you'd like.

Running late

We hold your slot for 15 minutes. After that, we may shorten your service or move you to the next open time so the next client isn't kept waiting.

Changing or cancelling

Moving or cancelling is free with at least 24 hours' notice. Text or call us and we'll sort it out, and you can always book a new time here.

Missed appointments

If you miss two appointments without telling us, we may ask for a small deposit on future bookings. We'll always let you know first.

Color and chemical services

Tell us about allergies or sensitive skin in your booking notes. For first-time color, we may do a quick patch test to keep your scalp safe.

Your details

We only use your name and number to manage your booking and send reminders. We never share or sell your information.

Final prices may vary slightly for very long or thick hair. Your stylist will always confirm the price with you before starting.

FAQ

Questions before your visit

Do I need to pay a deposit to book?

No. Booking online is free, and you pay at the salon after your service. We accept cash, GCash, Maya, and major cards.

The only time we ask for a deposit is if someone has missed two appointments without letting us know. Even then, we'll tell you before it applies.

How do I change or cancel my booking?

Text or call us at least 24 hours before your appointment and we'll move or cancel it for free. Your confirmation text has our number, so it's easy to find.

If something comes up on the day, still let us know. We'll do our best to fit you in at another time.

Will I get a reminder before my appointment?

Yes. As long as you tick the text updates box, you'll get a confirmation right after booking and a reminder the day before your visit.

Make sure your mobile number is correct when you book, since that's where both messages go. You can ask us to stop texts anytime.

Can I book more than one service or for someone else?

Yes. Book each service as its own appointment, one after the other. For example, a haircut at 2:00 PM and a manicure at 3:00 PM.

To book for a friend or family member, just use their name and mobile number so the confirmation and reminder go straight to them.

What if the time I want is already taken?

You'll see a message right away saying the time is booked, and nothing is saved. Just pick another time and try again.

Longer services like color or rebond need a bigger open window, so earlier times in the day are usually easier to get.
